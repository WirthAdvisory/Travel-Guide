About Us

We're Ross and Lisa — RL Approved Travel is us.

We love food, wine, travel, history, and architecture, and we've been traveling together for nearly two decades. This site is where we're putting all of it down: the restaurants worth the reservation, the ones that weren't, the churches worth wandering into, and the itineraries that actually worked for the pace we like to travel at.
