Guide to Firenze + Tuscany

Firenze + Tuscany

The David, the Uffizi, and cracker-thin pizza we went back for twice.

Wine⌄

Poliziano

Their Vino Nobile di Montepulciano is our house wine. We haven't been to visit the winery — yet.

Monsanto

Chianti Classico has been a favorite ever since a wine class back in college. First had Monsanto at Chicago's iconic Rose Bud — we've been drinking it ever since. Still haven't been to the winery.

Food⌄

Mangia Pizza

Where we ate twice — cracker-thin, crispy crust.

Map ↗

Gucci Osteria da Massimo Bottura

P.za della Signoria, 10

Fine dining — we booked thinking it would be super scene-y, but it was amazing. Best one-Michelin-star experience we've had anywhere in the world. Highly recommend for a high-end night in Florence.

Map ↗

Vini E Vecchi Sapori

Via dei Magazzini, 3/r

Super small, very highly rated — we weren't able to get in, all booked while we were there. On the list for next time.

Gelateria La Carraia

Piazza Nazario Sauro, 25/r

Recommended by the folks at Mangia Pizza. Didn't make it over last trip — saving it for next time.

See and Do⌄

The David

Must-see, practically indescribable in person. Buy tickets online.

The Uffizi

Unrivaled — you could spend days there. For a first trip, book a tour to at least see the highlights.

Tour ↗

Original Florence Walking Tour

One of three ArtViva walking tours we did on a previous trip — all pretty good.

Tour ↗

A Perfect Morning in Tuscany

The hike was a bit more intense than expected — don't wear flip-flops. Doesn't appear to be on ArtViva's site anymore, but they run tours in plenty of other cities too, worth checking.
